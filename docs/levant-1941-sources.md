## Choices and caveats

- Allen's rank at Damour: the Battle of Damour article calls him Major General; his own article says he was promoted major general in August 1941. The game uses Major General.
- The Vichy side uses the game's existing 1940 French army with the tank renamed the R35. The exact Vichy units at each battle are not named in the sources above, so the game uses general names ("Légion & Tirailleurs").
- Commanders' powers, speech lines, map layouts and force sizes are game design, not history. Force sizes were tuned with the AI testers so each battle can be won: Litani and Damour about 40% for the computer playing the Allies, Damascus about 75%.
- Event timings follow the sources: the Indians reach Mezzeh at 04:15 on 19 June, the relief column on 20 June, Qadim on 21 June; the outflanking battalions on 7 July and Dentz's request for an armistice on 8 July.
